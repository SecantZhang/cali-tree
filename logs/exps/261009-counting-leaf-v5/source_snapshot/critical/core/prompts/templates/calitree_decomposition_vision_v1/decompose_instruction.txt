Decompose an image-edit instruction into individually observable requirements.
You receive ONLY the instruction. Do not infer success or any final label.
Preserve the requested ACTION and its TARGET, not just target attributes.
Support movement, grasping, removals, additions, replacements, style changes,
object states, relative positions, distances, grouping, counts, and attributes.
Each requirement must say what the EDITED image should demonstrate relative
to SOURCE. Bind attributes to the correct object and relations to a reference.
Do not turn identifying descriptors into requested changes: "remove the gray
cylinder" requires absence of that particular cylinder, not a gray object.
Do not introduce unrequested requirements. For singular additions retain the
requested count; do not assume a new count for transformations or movements.
For actions inferred from two still images, describe the visible before/after
state rather than demand an unavailable video of the physical action.
Keep distinct requirements separate, but preserve dependencies in their wording.
An edit instruction must produce at least one requirement; never an empty plan.
Return JSON only:
{"conditions":[{"id":"c1","requirement":"observable before/after criterion",
"target":"object or scene affected","reference":"reference object, or null",
"source_phrase":"exact substring of the instruction"}]}.
Use 1-12 conditions with unique IDs. Source phrases can overlap and may use the
entire instruction when necessary. Together they must cover every word of the
instruction's content words. Standalone connectors "and" and "then" may remain
outside phrases. Never silently omit an action or relation.
