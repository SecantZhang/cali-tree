Propose at most TWO atomic typed-evidence-v2 transactions with up to FOUR actions each. Use original instruction, images, reference label, observed evidence, textual gradients and rejection diagnostics to investigate disagreement without forcing answers. Empty transactions is allowed if no faithful repair can be justified. A repeated mismatch can indicate a reference conflict; it never authorizes deleting an original requirement.
Models propose semantics; code builds graph edges. Every action has all schema fields; unused strings are "", unused arrays []. Use insert_support (existing after/before edge, unique new_check_id, requirement_id, question/criteria/binding, evidence_from and required_evidence_from, explicit readout_criteria/readout_required); replace_support or revise_support (target_id and full support semantics, optional revised readout); revise_readout (target_id=fulfillment, criteria, readout_required); remove_support (target_id, readout_criteria/readout_required); reorder_supports (support_order containing every support ID once); configure_activation (support target_id, activation_check earlier support ID or empty for always, activation_states, explicit readout_criteria/readout_required).
evidence_from supplies advisory ancestor context; required_evidence_from is its subset of genuinely needed known facts. For support changes activation_check/states specify the entire replacement activation; empty means always. Models MUST NOT supply raw parent, role, outcome, applicability, inference or graph fields. preserve one to three supports and fulfillment, immutable requirements/outcome/bindings and four-check/depth-four limits. All remaining context/prerequisite/activation references must point earlier after the complete transaction. Removal must revise dangling references in the same transaction. Insertion/reordering/splitting are validated atomically. Binding/criteria repairs may not invent completion requirements.
Uncertainty is not automatically wrong. Identify whether it is an unanswerable compound question, ambiguous correspondence, missing genuine evidence or unnecessary dependency. Inspect a final-state endpoint independently before comparing progress where appropriate. Use activation to skip a support only when the remaining evidence legitimately covers the requested result; explicitly compose unknown/skipped states in fulfillment. A targeted nested question should distinguish an actual unmet requirement, not duplicate a broad endpoint or make positive answers imply success. Novel, executable and faithful repairs are preferable to repeating a rejected idea.
Transport errors, interrupted attempts, invalid response schemas and missing dependency acknowledgments are not visual prediction errors. Do not weaken a question or invent an unsatisfied visual fact to repair an infrastructure failure. Treat image-grounded discoveries and reference-conflict diagnoses as hypotheses, not expected atomic answers.
