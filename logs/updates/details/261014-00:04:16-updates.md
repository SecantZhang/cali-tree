# 261014-00:04:16 — Four disjoint balanced twelve-case local replications

## Motivation

The user requested trying v4 on four different groups of twelve cases. Interpret
this in the established local-fitting setting: fit each group independently on
its own labels and compare with a matched flat baseline.

## Changes

- `run/calitree_four_groups.py`: deterministic source-disjoint balanced sampling,
  frozen protocol, matched one-attempt flat/rich calls, per-group isolated v4 fits,
  explicit infeasibility, metrics/report, immutable policy bundle and exact replay.
- `tests/unit/calitree/test_four_groups.py`: balance/determinism/exclusions,
  cross-group fit isolation, no fallback/resampling on infeasibility and invalid
  observations retained in full-case scoring denominator.
- `docs/calitree_four_groups.md`, `run/README.md`: scope, protocol and commands.
- `logs/exps/261013-four-groups-local-v4/`: experiment began Oct 13 and completed
  Oct 14; source/prompt/objective pins, 96 raw slots, independent policy bundle,
  results/report, audit, common-cohort analysis and G2 infeasibility diagnostic.

## Design decisions

Four groups of 12, four/reference class, all eight task categories in each group,
48 source-pixel-unique cases excluded from preceding 144 listed fit/holdout sources.
Official test tasks and AURORA-editor outputs only. No outcome-based sampling.

Keep model/profile, rich and flat prompts, v4 macro-F1 objective, class floor,
depth-two/three-split/two-source leaf support and monotonicity fixed. Use eight API
workers, one SOURCE-first response and one attempt per arm/case, no retries or voting.
Each fit uses only its group's usable observations/labels. An infeasible fit has
no policy and is not replaced, relaxed or silently pooled with another group.

## Follow-ups

Local three-class fitting works on three batches but not all; policy structure
varies across groups and the matched-response aggregate gains only one label
despite fitting on the references. Fixed two-correct-source floor implies 50%
per-class recall on these groups. This is fitting consistency, not generalization
or repeat robustness. No thresholds are relaxed after results and no optimizer
goal resumes.
