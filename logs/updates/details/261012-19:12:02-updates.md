## Design decisions

Intermediate progress and final qualification use separate thresholds. Baseline defects may
remain while a candidate improves, but must be repaired before final certification. The final
suite needs curated trusted references and new sources; repeating same-source model consensus
cannot provide those. Pixel hashes guard exact duplicates, while image lineage and annotation
quality remain trusted curated inputs. Even a passing finite suite supports only an empirical
claim in that suite/domain.

The prepared twelve-case v2 study has zero calls. Unrun preparations were refrozen for the final
media/freshness guards, with prior preparations/manifests preserved; historical live source and
results were not modified.
