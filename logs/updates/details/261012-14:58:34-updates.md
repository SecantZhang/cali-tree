## Design decisions

- Original-case correctness and model semantic audit govern acceptance. Contrast labels
  remain diagnostic and never feed selection. No synthetic images or live merge.
- Preserve IDs, source quotes, endpoints, weights and requirements. Protected-field
  problems are blocked instead of weakened. Five repeated draws use the existing 80%
  condition-consistency / 0.8-confidence policy; this is not a proof of robustness.
- Retain invalid/failed calls, reserve final capacity, freeze selection before fresh
  verification and never reopen search based on its outcome.
