## Design decisions
Use all 400 AURORA-model outputs, with 171 No / 143 Partial / 86 Yes and 50 tasks in
each of the eight categories. These remain released human-mean threshold labels,
not majority annotations. No downsampling or balancing. Source pixels reveal 394
source groups; two original training tasks share sources with test tasks, including
one under different filenames. Move the whole groups into test: 78 train / 322 test.
The original corpus and split files remain unchanged.

Use gpt-6-luna at temperature 0 and the adapter's fixed none reasoning setting for
all distinct stages, with no observer/high routes. Once means one pass of each
check per distinct program/row; baseline and changed candidate each get one pass.
One sparse candidate is allowed per training row. One initial audit and one audit
of a distinct candidate are distinct work, not repeated confirmation. Reporting
reuses measurements. One HTTP attempt per durable slot; no failed-slot retry.

Acceptance requires matching the overall dataset label; a mug-style candidate
that shifts Partial to Yes is rejected even when model audit findings improve.
This optimizes training leaves only and retains test rows for a later frozen
shared-parent merge phase. It does not claim robustness, transfer qualification,
merge eligibility or a globally unseen cohort. Historical completed studies and
source snapshots are preserved and still replay/verify under their old protocols.
