## Design decisions
Majority means more than half of votes for one edited output. 0/2 disagreement
is metadata, not a veto on a majority. The default minimum is three ratings and
is configurable; smaller panels remain pending. Original released means and
mean-derived labels are preserved separately from majority targets. No guesses
are made from means, and no model votes substitute for human annotations.
The derived directory deliberately has no metadata.jsonl because the original
AuroraBenchLoader recalculates targets from means. All original images, splits
and metadata remain untouched. Assets are checked for presence/nonempty content,
with the original asset manifest referenced by hash, not rehashed individually.
