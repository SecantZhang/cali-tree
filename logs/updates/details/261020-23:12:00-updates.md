## Design decisions

from 84 held source groups, source siblings kept together. Frozen validation 80
sources remain unused. No case/source IDs, object names or observed-example
lookups participate in inference. Runtime contains Boolean predicates, numeric
leaf probabilities, and frozen expert trees; training provenance is separate.

continuous direct score labels are 99/99, but lower-label action ties yield
95/99 routed labels in the reproduced fold, so regressors are ineligible.
The initial simplified screen required confidence >=.9 instead of executing
fallback. Per-threshold replay recovers eligible lower-threshold selectors.
Frozen study results remain immutable, with counterexamples/corrections saved
in the new audit.
