# 260729-15:23:02 — Preregister the progress/completion partial gate

## Motivation

The minimum-score calibrator is simple and transfers better than the richer alternatives
tested so far, but partial F1 remains weak. A rubric-semantic distinction has not yet been
tested directly: `partial` should mean that the requested edit visibly progressed while
full multi-dimensional completion remains absent.

## Changes

- `docs/experiments/rubric_lite_progress_completion_gate.json`: preregistered the exact
  architecture, three transparent progress definitions, task-grouped validation policy,
  candidate selection order, two-dataset advancement gate, and no-call constraint.

## Design decisions

The candidate retains one v4 judge call and fits only two thresholds. The upper boundary is
the existing minimum of all three rubric dimensions. Only the lower boundary changes to a
requested-edit progress score computed from change evidence and specification fidelity.
Minimum, mean, and maximum are the entire candidate set.

The progress definition is selected only on the 392-case EditInspector calibration half
and then held fixed for the 600-case ImagenHub transfer audit. Advancement requires at
least five points of partial-F1 improvement on both datasets, no balanced-accuracy
regression, and no more than two points of ordinary-accuracy regression. The untouched
EditInspector final half remains excluded.
