# 261019-12:23:56 — Preserve local accuracy with supported expert coverage and content-cached evidence

## Motivation

The user requested a way to preserve local trained accuracy while improving the gate's
ability to identify inputs covered by learned trees. V19's reference-guided judge lost
local accuracy by relying on task scope and having an LLM reinterpret conditional rules.

## Changes

- `evidence_coverage_v20.py`: bounded, supervised correctness guards for immutable experts. Regions require two distinct sources/instructions and zero local errors. Added declared task-direction features from affirmative compiled action verbs; inference has no source memberships or reference labels.
- `evidence_support_v21.py`: restrict accepted regions to their constant coarse semantic evidence predicates. Novel evidence defers; expert disagreements remain rejected.
- `complete_evidence_coverage.py`: reuse retained learned children only when selected-forest coverage has a local gap. One additional split-2 region has two-source/two-batch support and covers the final case without a new verdict or expert refit.
- `evidence_covered_judge.py`: original compiler/observer/interpreter pipeline, direct covered-tree execution, and exact regular fallback. Validates instruction/image/evidence bindings.
- `bound_evidence_cache.py`: instruction/image/model/prompt content keys, immutable upstream evidence slots, no human labels or final verdicts. Known raw inputs retain trained observations independently of request IDs.
- Added offline guard/support/completion runners, independent audits, raw-input cache verification, portable gate exports, results and documentation.

## Design decisions

Covered outputs are original expert predictions, not another prompted LLM verdict. Local
labels supervise where an expert is usable; original trees and labels remain unchanged.
The gate can defer for missing/invalid evidence, unseen tasks, absent support, semantic
novelty and expert disagreement. Cache lookups supply features, not classifications.

Initial two/three-literal screens left gaps. The bounded six-literal guard search covered
143/144 local slots. Increasing to eight literals did not close the remaining selected-
forest gap. Access to an already retained child added one supported region, yielding
144/144 deterministic tree outputs. No source-ID predicate or single-example exception
was introduced.

The semantic-support variants were screened on historical transfer diagnostics; their
results are exploratory development evidence, not an untouched confirmation study. All
formal gate fitting uses the corresponding original 48-row training pool. No final cases
or additional paid calls were used.

## Follow-ups

The measured local-preservation and coverage-improvement objective is achieved. This is
not universal coverage certification: 22 of 60 accepted transfer predictions are wrong,
and one transfer split regresses versus the regular judge. New inputs can produce new
extraction behavior; the evidence cache stabilizes known inputs under the pinned contract.
The implementation remains opt-in. Additional confirmation or paid fresh-extraction
studies require a separate scope; none was silently run.
