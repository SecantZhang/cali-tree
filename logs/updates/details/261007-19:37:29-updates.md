## Design decisions
The new design is an explicit adaptation: GEPA's documented default per-key selection differs from the proposed true multi-objective frontier. Textual feedback proposes changes, while measured forward executions establish their effects. Mutable nodes and repeated draws are not independent cases. Unvisited branches are untested, and skipping execution cannot erase required semantics. Frontier membership permits further exploration but does not establish eligibility as a robust final leaf.
