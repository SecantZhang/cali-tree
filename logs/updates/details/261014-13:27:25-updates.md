# 261014-13:27:25 — Instruction-grounded semantic distillation matches the learned local policy

## Motivation
The user rejected the v8/v9 ordinal-state rewrite as insufficiently semantic and requested a semantically meaningful transformation from the learned policy that preserves or improves accuracy on the same local cases. This work uses physical instruction-bound assertions and observed intermediate configurations rather than another naming scheme for quality grades.

## Changes
- Added thirty closed physical predicate questions and a label-blind cached-prose extractor, with true/false/NA/unknown states, bound physical questions and evidence.
- Added an instruction-only postcondition compiler: required-present/required-absent physical tests, supporting witnesses, identity witnesses and collateral roles. No observations or labels enter that compiler.
- Added eight concrete intermediate witnesses (reaching, object-like hand form, container tilt, approaching closure edges, changed style treatment, reversed end geometry, entity fragment). Mere unrelated limb motion cannot establish a positive result.
- Added exact deterministic proof and role features. Original-instance persistence, destination counterpart failure, relocation duplication, outcome attribute conflict and descriptive identification are separate. Unqualified appearance cautions are excluded from the final classifier.
- Added bounded teacher-assisted factual CART/DNF search variants with declared predicate polarities, requested-witness guards and two-source clause support. The final `instruction_semantic_rules.ObservableDNFCalibrator` fits teacher predictions and concept priorities; an optional original-label-refined arm is separate.
- Added versioned collectors, local benchmark/replay runners, independent audit/report, six concrete worked examples, case execution traces, docs/replay commands and thirty new unit/property tests. Historical failed and preliminary versions are preserved; production defaults are unchanged.

## Design decisions
The successful transformation is semantic re-induction from policy traces plus original instructions/evidence, not algebraic inversion of coefficients alone. Runtime features are physical states and exact properties of their instruction-bound conjunctions, with no ordered quality levels, scores, object-string checks, IDs or reference lookup table. Every positive clause must contain a requested physical witness (Yes) or requested/supporting physical witness (Partial). Each clause has at least two definite distinct source cases. There are at most three clauses per boundary and three atoms per clause. All three classes must recognize two training sources. Bounded enumeration is not advertised as a globally optimal search.

The primary final fit uses only original teacher outputs as fit targets; its target hashes and predictions were independently checked against the frozen numeric parent. Dataset labels score the student and fit only the separately named human_refined arm. Representation/ontology choices used exploratory local errors and references, so the overall method-development process is not gold-blind or held-out.

## Follow-ups
The physical extractor can misread cached prose, the instruction compiler can omit/overconstrain conditions, and twelve cases underdetermine a rubric. Some group rules omit safeguards another group needs. Matching local accuracy with lower coverage is not held-out transfer, causal correctness or repeat robustness. The current method is opt-in; freeze it before fresh-source validation or production promotion. No new image-level factual validation is claimed.
