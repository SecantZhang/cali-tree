## Design decisions

Compile one common label-free decomposition, then independently optimize binary and fulfillment scoring views with the
same anchored observer and allowances. Binary means full endpoint score 1 with all/some/none aggregation; fulfillment
means >=.9 satisfied, <=.1 nonsatisfied, intermediate partial. This isolates aggregation under a common grader, not a
literal replay of the historical Boolean prompt. Keep confidence/consistency gates separate; a resolved interval label
does not certify its missing atom. Semantic auditing stays disabled; quotes and preserved weights are not semantic proof.
Four checks, fifteen rounds, fixed Luna family, one HTTP attempt and one outcome-blind transport recovery per report.
