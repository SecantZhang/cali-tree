## Design decisions
Only AURORA-editor outputs and gpt-6-luna settings are used. Other source rows are
ordinary independently judged examples; no alternate-editor outputs or voting.
Each bank/case is measured once. The selected observation bank has no case-ID
checks, but a blind model portability audit alone does not establish transfer.
The fitting mug and all twelve diagnostic sources are excluded from six training
development rows and six heldout rows. H01-H50 reserved sources are not consumed.

The Pareto front tracks fitting agreement, development macro and worst-class
accuracy, coverage and simplicity. Selected steps cannot reduce fitting,
development pooled or development worst-class accuracy. Selection freezes before
heldout measurement. The goal remains active because heldout regression defeats
qualification; the full twelve-case phase has not been promoted/run.

The next-version foundation predicts aggregate human scores from observations
using bounded-depth trees and source-disjoint CV. IDs/instruction text/gold labels
are not executable features. Missingness is distinct from zero fulfillment. This
is statistical label prediction, not recovery of annotator votes or a physical
truth claim. Minimum eighteen valid rows and at least three sources per leaf
prevent a one-point lookup from being presented as a generalized learned rule.
