## Design decisions

- Use shared rules plus nine check-family refinements. Optimize overall labels
  separately from the image observer using the same Luna-medium model.
- Preserve original instructions and mean-derived references; no atomic human
  check labels or annotator vote reconstruction.
- Deploy distilled compiler policy on unseen instructions; never transplant a
  fitting case's bound checks. Reject semantically defective check sets before
  image measurement, while allowing structural repair from their audit issues.
- New experimental artifacts do not bypass or rewrite the v7 immutable weight
  ledger. Baseline uses equal requested-check mass with fixed .1/.9 bounds.
- Cache semantic requests across arms/stages, including failed slots. Comments
  do not create a new executable check-set identity. Unknowns remain unresolved
  when they can affect the output; confidence is separate.
- Exclude real measurement records and conservative earlier cohorts, but ignore
  offline corpus inventories and untouched-ID lists. No consumed-case fallback.
