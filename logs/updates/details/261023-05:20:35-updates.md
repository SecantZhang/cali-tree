## Design decisions

Region binding scores, caption affinities and NLI entailment are fallible model
views. They do not certify image facts. Missing detections stay unavailable;
uncertainty never becomes a failure witness. NLI concerns what a textual
explanation claims, not what the image necessarily depicts. The additive rubric
retains the boosted score explicitly instead of claiming standalone distillation.

All comparisons keep the same276 primary cases,84 pixel-source groups and five
source-held folds, excluding every held-source sibling. Original99 cases remain
protected in actual fitting/inference, with144 original slots checked. The bound
evidence gate stays unchanged at273/276. The direct physical expert controls
are not protected and demonstrably lose originals; they are not serving candidates.

The richer evidence does not improve the best accuracy. Retain v72, rather than
promoting a larger tied pool or narrowing the benchmark. The active235/276 goal
remains incomplete. These are reviewed development results after repeated model
selection, with no new reserved-source judging.
