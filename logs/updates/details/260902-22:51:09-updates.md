# 260902-22:51:09 — Pilot-first Cali-Tree safety guards

## Motivation

The first behavioral GPT-4o benchmark attempts were too large to diagnose efficiently and
stalled in the gateway path. A bounded live pilot was needed to validate the complete
leaf-optimize, merge, parent-optimize, route, parse, and evaluate path before resuming the
benchmark. The pilot then exposed real accuracy and reporting regressions.

## Changes

- `run/run_imagenhub_calitree.py`: added `--pilot`, selecting 12 training and 24 held-out
  ImagenHub cases while bounding optimization to one step and three merge attempts. Added a
  configurable per-request timeout, with 90 seconds as the runner default.
- `vejudge/interface/node_vejudge/lm_engine_node.py`,
  `vejudge/interface/node_calibration/calitree_nodes.py`, and
  `web/src/nodes/paramSchemas.ts`: carried `health_check` and `timeout` through the serialized
  engine configuration into live Cali-Tree engines.
- `vejudge/lm_engine/openai_compat.py`: a rate-limited primary now fails over immediately when
  a mirror exists, while a single endpoint retains bounded retry behavior.
- `vejudge/core/calibration/calitree.py`: failure-mode leaves now require matching held-out
  support and no regression against the best flat prompt before routing. Promoted singletons
  inherit that guard. Locally accepted parents are re-evaluated on the complete validation
  split and must strictly improve balanced accuracy without reducing raw accuracy before
  becoming the accumulated root or receiving traffic.
- `vejudge/core/calibration/calitree.py`: missing/invalid predictions now count as explicit
  errors in accuracy, recall, and confusion metrics instead of disappearing from class
  denominators.
- `vejudge/interface/node_calibration/calitree_nodes.py`: recovered GPT-4o's explicit prose
  scorecards and “fully/partly/not present” final verdicts. The parser now recovers all 30
  previously invalid pilot outputs in an offline replay.
- `docs/calitree.md` and `run/README.md`: documented the pilot workflow, held-out routing/root
  guards, and the evidence boundary for the pilot result.

## Pilot evidence

- The initial 36-case live pilot completed in about 7.5 minutes (108 judge, 10 optimizer, and
  four embedding calls). It exposed three failures: unvalidated leaf routing reduced held-out
  accuracy from 83.33% to 79.17%; a locally accepted merge could replace the root on a tiny
  guard; and invalid prose outputs inflated class metrics by disappearing from denominators.
- The guarded cached pilot completed with all 24 held-out predictions valid. Both accepted
  local parents tied rather than beat the full validation baseline, so neither became root;
  all under-supported leaves were blocked. Cali-Tree therefore safely matched the plain judge:
  accuracy 83.33%, balanced accuracy 61.90%, macro-F1 57.44%.
- This is a safety/tie result on a deliberately small pilot, not evidence that Cali-Tree beats
  the baseline. The larger benchmark remains paused until a pilot configuration demonstrates a
  real held-out gain.
