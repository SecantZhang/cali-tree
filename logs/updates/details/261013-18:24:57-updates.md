# 261013-18:24:57 — Constrained reusable-rule local calibration

## Motivation

The user explicitly requested fitting and scoring the same twelve cached evidence
cases, while preventing case-specific calibration rules. This intentionally
measures local fitting accuracy rather than disjoint-batch transfer.

## Changes

- `critical/core/calibration/local_evidence_rules.py`: Calibrator subclass with
  exact search over a fixed categorical predicate grammar; max depth two/three
  splits; at least two distinct source cases per learned leaf; exhaustive ordinal
  monotonicity; fully fulfilled Yes and decisive No/unresolved guards; portable
  validation and anonymous feature-conflict diagnostics.
- `run/calitree_local_calibration.py`: offline preflight/fit/replay CLI, pinned
  same-case caches/labels/code, immutable registry policy, exact refit verification
  and baseline comparison report. No LM engine or new image/model calls.
- `tests/unit/calitree/test_local_evidence_rules.py`: local fitting, text/entity
  renaming invariance, no count/duplication signatures, metadata/predicate rejection,
  source-support requirements, monotonicity, conflicts and portable refitting.
- `docs/calitree_local_evidence_calibration.md`, `run/README.md`: constraints,
  usage and explicit limits of local fitting.
- `logs/exps/261013-local-reusable-rules-v1/`: frozen experiment, fitted policy,
  machine-readable results, report, replay, 48-variant audit and interpretation.

## Design decisions

Only anonymous worst core/constraint fulfillment states and source integrity can
enter learned predicates. Raw instructions validate provenance only. No text
vocabulary, object names, case IDs, task/editor metadata, exact counts, singleton
exceptions or arbitrary numeric thresholds are allowed in the executable tree.
Training source groups enforce distinct-source support but cannot be predicates.

Search maximizes local correctness and then favors fewer split nodes and fewer
departures from the fixed complete-only policy on the finite evidence domain.
This does not claim semantic generality from syntax alone. The original leaf
optimizer and previous experiments remain unchanged; no optimizer goal resumes.
