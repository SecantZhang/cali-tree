## Design decisions

The official published EditInspector aggregate is the target; all three individual ratings
are retained for agreement analysis. The 80-case development slice is a strict subset of
the 392-case materialization, and the live confirmation includes only the other 312 cases.

The yes-only partial verifier reduced development accuracy from 60.00% to 56.25%. A second
checkpoint-reused diagnostic on the primary no/partial cases reached 70.00% but left
partial F1 at 17.39%. Neither verifier policy advanced to confirmation.

The frozen perfect-evidence rule advanced because it reached 100% accuracy at 50% coverage
on development. On untouched confirmation it reached 97.62% on 168/312 cases (53.85%
coverage), Wilson 95% CI 94.04–99.07%. Full-coverage accuracy was only 58.65% and partial
F1 only 6.67%; the accepted set predicts only yes. The documented claim is therefore a
high-precision selective yes detector, not solved three-class calibration.
