## Design decisions

Forward results: regular 20/36; v27 21/19/21; v28 21/19/20; verification-only
21/19/22. All three banks are retained, with no best-bank selection. In bank 3,
the verifier rejects N022's wrong Partial route and uses the correct regular No.
This is one new correct prediction and no lost correct predictions versus v27.

The correction arm has the same forward accuracy but adds a wrong No on N024
in banks 1 and 2, where both reference and old outcome demonstrate that greater
coverage does not improve the answer. This worsens accepted precision, macro F1
and ordinal error. The serving option therefore defaults to the previously
declared verification-only arm. The mode was selected after this comparison;
the underlying model artifacts and both arms were frozen beforehand. No clauses,
weights, expert rules or regions were refitted to forward references.

The result is an incremental empirical improvement, not strong statistical
evidence. Sources have older exposure but are excluded from fitting these fixes.
The 36 cases now count as development evidence for future modifications. Bank 2
still trails regular, and conditional accepted accuracy remains imperfect.

This default always obtains a regular judgment. The original pipeline is not
silently switched to the more expensive serving path. The safe merge algorithm
retains semantic obligations and two reductions, rather than promising v28's
more aggressive 44-region result. These are deliberate accuracy/cost/size
tradeoffs, not a guarantee of error-free future inference.
