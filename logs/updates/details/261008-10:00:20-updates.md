## Design decisions

The reference label selects an investigation, not an atomic ground truth. Proposed extent checks are hypotheses until evaluated; the chalk live artifacts are unchanged and are not claimed to have gained a verified new observation. Negative nested evidence is usable when its parent passes. Required unknowns remain unresolved; supporting checks never earn completion credit.

Audit-rejected executable parents can supply a single diagnostic draw per hash. Those scores cannot enter the screen/confirmation frontiers, qualify a candidate or replace an audited incumbent. Repairs still require structural validation, their own label-blind semantic audit, fresh confirmation and frozen final verification. Invalid compilation receives no fabricated feedback.

The runtime container remains v3 because it already saves the complete checker template and execution identity. The new checker contract has a distinct identity/hash. Promoting a historical decomposition is explicit and requires a fresh audit; saved v1 programs are never silently interpreted as nested programs.

Default reviewer choice is the primary model. Other families require explicit engine factories and frozen routes sharing the same durable scope. There is no provider fallback, automatic failed-slot repair or new live expenditure.
