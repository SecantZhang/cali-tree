## Design decisions

Agreement is a known modal result count divided by five. Missing/unknown draws cannot inflate agreement by shrinking the denominator. The final-answer thresholds are nested: 39/50 at least 60%, 36/50 at least 80%, 23/50 at 100%. Full decision-set vectors reach 35/50, 30/50 and 17/50. Human labels do not define agreement and never enter requests. No direct raw-prompt comparison, decomposition refitting, relabeling or training was performed. Eight workers share the existing 336-call/344,064-completion-token cap; failures retain reservations. Actual successful new usage: 417,789 input / 46,949 output tokens; ledger 56,165 output tokens including failures/interruption.
