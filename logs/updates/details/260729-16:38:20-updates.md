# 260729-16:38:20 — Preregister multi-lane human review

## Motivation

The exact-evidence Rubric-Lite policy provides a high-precision automated `yes` lane but
refers almost every `no` and `partial` case. A simple extension should be tested before
adding another model, router, verifier, or dataset-specific calibrator.

## Changes

- `docs/experiments/rubric_lite_multilane_selective_gate.json`: froze three semantic `no`
  candidates, three semantic `partial` candidates, the exact-`yes` baseline, input hashes,
  development selection rules, confirmation gates, and stopping rules.
- `docs/calitree.md`: recorded the zero-call experiment and prohibited policy inputs.

## Design decisions

The policy may inspect only the existing three Rubric-Lite v4 scores and calibrated label.
It may not use dataset, editor, task, or instruction identity. Candidate lanes must transfer
on both ImagenHub and EditInspector development data; a lane is omitted rather than fitted
to a failing dataset. Confirmation remains uninspected at candidate level unless the
combined development policy exceeds 90% auto accuracy, preserves baseline precision, and
adds at least five coverage points on both datasets.
