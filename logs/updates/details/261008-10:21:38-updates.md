## Design decisions

The first attempt completed 38 calls but generated invalid decomposition ancestry. Preserve it. To exercise repair independently of fresh compilation, explicitly promote the historical original label-free decomposition and give the controlled attempt the remaining 112 calls / 188,790 completion tokens. It uses the same images/model, never retries a failed slot, and cannot initiate a chain of corrective attempts or bypass a provider stop.

Compiler guidance was clarified from the structural error only, not retuned using final outcomes. The corrected guidance was tested offline, but the controlled attempt used a saved seed rather than claiming a successful second compilation measurement. All final draws follow frozen selections. No final outcome triggers another optimization or candidate replacement.
