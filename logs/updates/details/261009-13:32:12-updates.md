## Design decisions

Keep original requirements, bindings, applicability and counting immutable. Literal quotes and model necessity
explanations are grounding evidence, not semantic proof; an auditor is not reintroduced. Keep 3,600 calls / 4,608,000
completion tokens, allocating 107 search and 42 final calls per scope and reserving 1,008 final calls. Every slot has
one HTTP attempt. Recovery is a declared new measurement with original failures visible; it does not release stop
latches or turn repeats into new cases. Crops, annotation relabeling, parent learning and UI are not included.
