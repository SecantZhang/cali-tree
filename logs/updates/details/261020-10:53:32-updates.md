# 261020-10:53:32 — Merge semantically similar regions across experts under retention constraints

## Motivation
Relax exact region equivalence to share semantic applicability across experts,
while retaining local accuracy and protecting coverage decisions.

## Changes
- `critical/core/calibration/semantic_region_merge_v28.py`: bounded pair search on
  shared semantic literals and task scopes; same-verdict cross-expert proposals;
  common-condition parents; local-source and routing checks; contributor-aware
  runtime; deduplicated pooled/per-task support and containment checks.
- `run/calitree_semantic_region_merge_v28.py`: offline three-split study and audit
  reproducing the search, source hashes, support counts and cached raw inference.
- `tests/unit/calitree/test_semantic_region_merge_v28.py`: eleven cases for genuine
  widening, cross-expert sharing, local retention, blocked new acceptances,
  ignored regression labels, contributor gating, serialization, deduplication,
  preserved disagreement veto and bounded configuration.
- Documentation, result/audit JSON, portable examples and runner usage.

## Design decisions
Eligibility requires the same verdict, different contributor sets, overlapping
structural task scopes, literal Jaccard at least 0.5 and a shared substantive
semantic/outcome condition. The parent intersects conditions and unions permitted
task scopes and contributor sets. Candidates must preserve each child's local
support and every recorded accept/defer decision and accepted verdict.

The full v24 expert bank and rejection logic remain unchanged. A final region
also requires a supported match from one of its contributing experts, so an
unrelated expert cannot authorize it. Region conditions may broaden; old regions
are not kept as a hidden executable veto. The v28 wrapper makes empirical
validation explicit even though it reuses v27's generic shared-condition storage.
This does not merge or retrain expert trees or change inference defaults.

Known development probes supervise routing retention via frozen decisions;
their human reference labels are not used in the search. Local labels still
validate support. These are regression data, not a fresh generalization test.

Earlier drafts and source snapshots are retained under the base and `-r1`
experiment directories. Final `-r2` includes contributor-aware routing and the
validated fixture/protocol fixes; it is the audited and sealed result.
