# 261020-14:26:19 — Develop and verify guarded semantic merges and regular-aware expert decisions

## Motivation

The rejected-case analysis found Yes shortcuts that ignore available goal
failures, unconstrained voting over differently competent experts, and genuine
upstream grounding errors. The active goal is to improve gates and merges while
retaining local accuracy. This update targets policy/routing failures using
cached evidence; it does not change upstream observations or reference labels.

## Changes

- `critical/core/calibration/semantic_guarded_merge_v32.py`: short per-verdict
  support clauses with protected correct positives; symbolic preservation of
  goal/outcome/materiality/uncertainty distinctions during merges; child-support
  retention and no new fitting-set false acceptance.
- `decision_warrant_gate_v33.py`: affirmative No/Yes outcome warrants with a
  matching expert and support from multiple correct sources. Retained as an
  experimental control because greater coverage does not improve every bank.
- `semantic_fallback_router_v34.py`: compact conditional correction regions
  with at least two beneficial sources and no observed fitting errors.
- `regular_aware_gate_v35.py`: verdict verification may consult the regular
  judge's predicted label; No overriding Partial additionally requires explicit
  incidental change or wrong direction/target, avoiding an absence-only shortcut.
- `critical/core/optimization/prompt/calitree/regular_aware_judge_v35.py`:
  optional inference wrapper reusing cached bound evidence and one unchanged
  regular judgment. Separates original model response/provenance from the policy
  verdict. Budget/provider stops propagate; failed calls are not retried.
- Versioned runners v32/v33/v34/v35: 16-source cross-fitting, frozen final
  models and complete per-case routes. Every sibling output is excluded from
  all fitting stages for its held source. Existing production defaults unchanged.
- `calitree_guarded_forward_v32.py`, `collect_calitree_guarded_forward_v32.py`:
  prepare/freeze 36 legacy cached sources excluded from all new fitting. The

- `verify_calitree_guarded_development_v35.py`: independent exact refits,
  held-source replay, local retention and accepted merge proof checks. Freezes
  supplementary v35 policies before any new regular collection.
- `docs/calitree_guarded_development.md`, `run/README.md`: results, limitations,
  reproduction entry points and the changed inference cost profile.

## Design decisions

Only declared semantic predicates and model predictions enter runtime clauses;
source IDs and human labels are not runtime routing inputs. All correct local
and prior accepted support is protected during fitting. An inseparable training
error is disclosed rather than hidden behind an example-specific exception.

The first v32 setup failed before fitting due to a local variable shadowing the
previous merger module. The corrected study is `261020-guarded-generalization-v32-r1`.
The strengthened v33 and corresponding forward preparation use their `-r1`
directories. Earlier prototype outputs/source snapshots are retained. Original
v27/v28, pilot, ungated and diagnosis artifacts remain unchanged.

The final v35 arm scores 59/57/56 out of 80, versus v27 57/50/50, v28 56/50/47
and regular 53. These are exploratory source-held-out checks after inspecting
the pilot, not untouched generalization results. Bank 1 loses one Partial
correctness relative to v27 and has slightly lower macro F1 despite higher
accuracy. With 60 No references, always-No scores 75% accuracy; no claim is
made that these models beat that baseline on raw accuracy.

Full v32 merges retain only two safe reductions, 63 to 61 regions. The old
v28's more aggressive 44-region compression is not used as evidence of safe
semantic widening. The new v35 wrapper always calls regular, including for
covered inputs, so it should not be deployed as a cost-saving replacement
without separately assessing that tradeoff.
