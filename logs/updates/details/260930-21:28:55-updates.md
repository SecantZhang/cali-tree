## Design decisions
Class-balance weights determine split impurity and leaf loss; ordinary case mass and independent source/instruction group counts constrain support. Fraction features distinguish failure proportions without encoding entity/case/category identity. Guarded overwrites require two distinct source groups whose targets support that correction, plus a penalty for changing the fixed rubric. Greedy Gini growth is followed by bottom-up balanced-loss and leaf-count pruning. Repeat disagreement enters training validation selection. This is not globally optimal tree search or automatic concept induction.

The fixed rubric can be selected rather than forcing a worse learned program. The later evaluation cohort was reused after earlier inspection, making results exploratory despite training-only choices. One independent positive training case remains a material limitation.
