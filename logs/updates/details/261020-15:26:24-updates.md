## Design decisions

N102 requests removal of the car in front of a bus. The tree downgrades to
Partial on a material-collateral-change branch, while regular and the reference
are Yes. The bus's lettering/details visibly change; this is a materiality/rubric
mismatch rather than a fabricated disappearance. The responsible a7821fa6… leaf
was fitted on two Partial examples from G3. The raw bank also contains a Yes
expert, but its no-collateral-deformation support guard fails on this evidence.

Disabling the entire rule/leaf would lose correct local decisions, including
N135 whose only supported route is Partial and whose regular judgment is Yes.
The fitted bank-2 veto instead applies only to Partial-versus-Yes disagreements
on removal tasks with all identity checks fulfilled. It retains N073's correct
Partial with uncertain identity and N135's spatial Partial. It is a learned
relative-competence filter, not a claim that identity proves goal completion.

The initial prototype also learned a bank-1 veto that preserved recorded local
judgments but would reject two correct prior routes if regular changed its label
to Yes. The final r1 procedure augments protected prior routes with every
possible regular label. Bank 1's harmful example is then inseparable under the
bounded grammar and no veto is installed; bank 2's veto remains feasible.
No complex example-specific exception is added.

The full development bank-2 score improves 19→20/36 and tree coverage decreases
8→7, retaining four correct tree decisions. Banks 1/3 remain 21/36 and 22/36.
All other recent development decisions are unchanged. The 36 cases are now
development data for this revision. Holding out the sole harmful source prevents
the clause from being learned, leaving bank 2 at 19/36. This is an explicitly
limited development correction, not fresh evidence of generalization.
