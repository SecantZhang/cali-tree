## Design decisions

The full-coverage result remains primary. V4 achieved 81.33% accuracy versus v2's 82.83%;
the paired task-bootstrap interval includes zero, but v4 is also lower on balanced accuracy
and macro F1, so it is described as a simpler near-accuracy alternative rather than a
better classifier. The perfect-evidence policy accepted only 95 ImagenHub cases and was
51.58% accurate. Leaving it in the base artifact would be unsafe, even though the identical
target-blind predicate previously confirmed at 97.62% on EditInspector. Its deployment
scope is therefore encoded in a separate artifact rather than inferred from dataset
identity at runtime.

the 90% full-coverage or partial-class goal. Any future review policy must pass matched
cross-dataset development gates before being attached to a general artifact.
