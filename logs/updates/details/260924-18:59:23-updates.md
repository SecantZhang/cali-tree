# Fifty-case repeated-evaluation GEPA comparison

- Added a 50-case runner selecting distinct AURORA test-split tasks outside the
  previous 100-case repair sample, with frozen image and rubric hashes, the
  same five-proposal budget for single-draw and repeated GEPA arms, a $30
  estimated list-cost cap, and 15 fresh final judgments per case and arm.
- Added a bounded parallel resume orchestrator using separate trajectories
  and the existing exact call checkpoints. A transient TLS failure during final
  evaluation was recovered without replacing completed final responses.
- Completed 500 mutation rounds and 1,500 fresh final judgments. Single-draw
  repair scored 475/750; repeated repair scored 472/750. The paired task-bootstrap
  95% interval for repeated minus single accuracy was [-9.2, +8.8] points.
  Estimated total API list cost was $10.383903.
- Independently verified 7,417 unique provider responses, 50 distinct tasks,
  500 prompt-decision lineages, final calls after prompt freeze, exact image
  hashes, target-free judge requests, and the final score totals.
- Added the experiment report and reproduction notes in
  `docs/experiments/robust_prompt_repair_50.md`.
