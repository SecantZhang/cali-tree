## Motivation

A frozen development variant improved image-backed grading with labeled training
image pairs and adjacent source/edited and query/reference markers. Establish
its behavior on untouched AURORA sources and expose the tested method through
the existing decomposition abstraction without changing defaults.

## Design decisions

Findings and the decision share one vision call; independent observation stages
are explicitly unsupported for this strategy. Training examples are optional;
reference annotations never describe the query. Retrieval parameters match the
frozen prototype. Examples use one source per human class, and generic IDs do
not reveal task/editor identity to the model. Existing decomposition defaults
are unchanged. The method is experimental: references tied their matched
control on fresh raw accuracy, and perfect agreement remains unachieved.
