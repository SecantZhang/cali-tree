## Motivation

The user confirmed that “15 runs” means fifteen repair rounds. The previous chalk insertion only reached confirmation in the last of two rounds, leaving no opportunity to repair that newly added check after its aggregate prediction failed.

## Changes

- Robust search accepts an explicit round schedule, early stopping on complete qualifying confirmation, and failed-confirmation follow-up. Legacy defaults remain the original `(1, 2)` schedule.
- Typed optimization defaults to fifteen rounds, one parent batch and up to two proposals per round. `max_rounds` is bounded to 1–15 and forwarded through the optimizer factory.
- A failed confirmation becomes the persistent next repair parent, with its full report and rejection reasons in feedback. It does not lose priority to the cheaper original seed merely because their one-draw screen scores tie. Empty/rejected proposals continue the loop. Duplicate evaluated programs are recorded and not resampled for another chance at acceptance.
- Search stops early on audited, structurally eligible full confirmation success; otherwise it exhausts fifteen rounds or reports an enforced budget/provider stop. Lineage records planned batches, rounds started/completed and parent-choice reasons. Structural fallback records its failure separately from the round-limit stop.
- Typed proposal guidance explains failed inserted-component repair without assigning an atomic expected failure from the reference label or weakening original requirements.
- The runner's new `typed-evidence-chalk-pilot-v2` configuration freezes fifteen rounds and stopping/follow-up settings. Historical manifests dispatch their explicit two-round schedule/settings; source snapshots and final observations are retained. Existing call/token/final-reservation ceilings are not increased.
- Architecture and usage documentation explain provisional confirmation success, independent frozen final verification and possible budget-limited search.

## Design decisions

The user requested longer bounded optimization, not fifteen repeats of an unchanged candidate. Each round can discover, critique, propose and evaluate new immutable programs. The confirmation that says an inserted program is unsuccessful is valid training feedback; fresh final outcomes remain excluded from optimization.

An audited check can legitimately pass while the aggregate disagrees. The feedback investigates missing evidence, criteria, binding or composition rather than manufacturing a negative answer. A complete qualifying confirmation can end search early; local robustness still requires the subsequent independent final schedule.

Preserve legacy defaults and explicit historical schedule dispatch. Fifteen repair rounds can require many model calls, so existing call/token budgets and provider stops take precedence. Do not claim all fifteen rounds executed when an allowance interrupts a round.
