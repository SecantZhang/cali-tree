## Motivation

Compact probabilistic calibration did not improve leakage-safe partial F1. The remaining
simple hypothesis is that the v4 rubric needs a stronger visual judge, not a more flexible
post-hoc model. The candidate and advancement rule must be frozen before billable calls to
avoid choosing metrics, thresholds, or models after seeing results.

- `docs/calitree.md`: linked the preregistration and its advancement thresholds.

## Design decisions

Only the image model changes from `gpt-4.1-mini` to `gpt-4.1`. Both arms use the same
source-before-edited ordering, v4 prompt, temperature zero, 512 completion-token cap,
80-case development partition, and five-fold seed-44 two-cutpoint calibration. No
verifier, optimizer, embedding, routing, editor feature, or instruction feature is
allowed.

The candidate advances only if out-of-fold partial F1 improves by at least five percentage
points while accuracy remains at least 78% and balanced accuracy remains at least 66.65%.
All three conditions are required.
