# 260729-15:06:41 — Correct task-grouped Rubric-Lite validation

## Motivation

The larger gpt-4.1 judge did not improve partial, and compact probabilistic calibrators did
not survive nested validation. The remaining simple rubric-learning question was whether a
compact monotone combination of the three existing semantic scores could transfer better
than their minimum without adding model calls or architectural complexity.

## Changes

- `vejudge/core/calibration/rubric_lite.py`: added deterministic task-grouped cutpoint
  cross-validation, balancing label/case load while guaranteeing every requested fold has
  at least one complete task.
- `vejudge/interface/node_calibration/rubric_lite_nodes.py`: enabled task grouping by
  default and recorded the setting in the fitted calibrator.
- `workflows/examples/rubric_lite_editinspector_calibrated.json`: made the non-leaking
  grouped validation policy explicit.
- `web/src/nodes/paramSchemas.ts`: exposed the grouped-validation switch with the safe
  default.
- `docs/experiments/rubric_lite_monotone_feature_audit.json` and `docs/calitree.md`:
  recorded the corrected 30-candidate, two-dataset negative audit and retained the
  minimum-score deployment rule.
- Backend and frontend tests cover task isolation, non-empty folds, and default parameter
  registration.

## Design decisions

The runtime remains one global v4 judgment, the minimum of its three visible scores, and
two cutpoints. No new model call, tree, embedding, router, classifier, editor feature, or
instruction feature was added.

The audit evaluated 30 convex monotone scalars on 392 EditInspector calibration cases and
600 ImagenHub cases from 75 disjoint tasks. Specification fidelity won only on
EditInspector. On corrected five-fold task-grouped ImagenHub validation, the minimum
retained higher partial F1 (33.78% versus 32.65%). The ImagenHub-selected
change/preservation mean also failed to transfer back to EditInspector.

The first experimental grouped allocator packed tasks into only three folds and left two
empty, producing an invalid favorable result for specification fidelity. That result was
caught before commit and withdrawn. The production allocator now seeds every fold before
balancing normalized label/case load; tests assert task isolation and non-empty folds.

The untouched 391-case EditInspector final partition remains unused.
