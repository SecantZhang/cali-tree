## Design decisions

Coverage uses outcome kinds/polarities, identity requirements and single-versus-compound
structure. Runtime routing sees no outcomes, labels, example IDs or images. Support is
learned offline from the original local training cases; it is not a generalization
claim. A reference rule needs two correctly served sources and distinct instructions,
and zero known same-scope counterexamples in the local pool. Only one policy's rules
are supplied at a time; oversized references fall back rather than being truncated.

Covered requests use the regular judge with advisory rules and the original response
schema. Uncovered requests are byte-equivalent at the prompt/payload/schema level to
the regular judge and reuse its exact response in the comparison. Fresh compilation
is optional; this experiment reuses frozen compiler-v3 programs and has no observer,
interpretation, refitting, relabeling, retry or reserved-final-set call.
