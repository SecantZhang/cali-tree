Apply these semantic grading/reliability rules in order; otherwise retain the boosted teacher.
A review/defer rule selects an independent image judge; it never turns uncertainty itself into failure.
Shared rubric definitions:
rubric_no_ready: Every requested requirement has coherent failure evidence: no goal-directed progress, only incidental change, a wrong endpoint/target, or loss of an essential instance, with an explicitly failed outcome/identity witness.
rubric_partial_ready: At least one same requirement has coherent goal-directed progress with a known goal witness, or an achieved endpoint with a witnessed shortfall; complete success is not established for every requirement.
rubric_yes_ready: Every same requirement has an achieved endpoint, all applicable physical outcome witnesses established, no endpoint gap, accepted identity, at most incidental collateral changes, and coherent evidence.
1. IF the boosted teacher proposes no AND All of the requested requirements have collateral materiality = material unrequested change. AND The boosted and regular visual judges propose different available grades. AND The explicit semantic rubric does not establish the boosted proposal; this does not prove it wrong. AND The explicit semantic rubric establishes the regular judge proposal. THEN use the independent regular visual judgment.
2. IF the boosted teacher proposes partial AND The boosted and regular visual judges propose different available grades. AND The explicit semantic rubric does not establish the boosted proposal; this does not prove it wrong. AND The explicit semantic rubric establishes the regular judge proposal. AND The boosted proposal has normalized model support below 0.65; support is not calibrated correctness. THEN use the independent regular visual judgment.
