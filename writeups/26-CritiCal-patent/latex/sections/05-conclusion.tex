\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \critical, a modular calibration framework that aligns multimodal video-editing judges with human evaluators without retraining or fine-tuning the underlying foundation models. By combining counterbalanced native-video and contact-sheet frame perceivers (Stage~1), multi-turn adversarial debate with independent media-grounded verification (Stage~2), and regularized statistical fusion paired with reliability-gated selective prediction (Stage~3), \critical addresses incomplete single-model perception, position bias, and unverified reasoning in a single auditable pipeline. On VE-Bench, \critical reaches 85.40\% full-coverage human agreement---outperforming both the strongest individual video judge and a two-expert video router---and achieves up to 93.40\% selective accuracy on accepted comparisons when deferring high-risk cases for closer review.
