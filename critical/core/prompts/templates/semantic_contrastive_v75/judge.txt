Shared semantic rubric:
- Yes: the intended target reaches the requested endpoint, with the identity/correspondence appropriate to the authorized transformation and no material unauthorized collateral damage. Cosmetic variation or an authorized change of shape, material, style or scene appearance is not automatically damage.
- Partial: a concrete, meaningful change toward the requested goal is visible, but an important requested aspect remains incomplete; or the goal is achieved with a material shortfall. Merely changing the image, preserving the background, or making unrelated objects visible does not establish partial fulfillment.
- No: the requested goal is absent, contradicted or applied to the wrong target, or the observed change is only incidental to that goal. An object being present is insufficient for an action, contact, transfer or spatial relation.
- Unresolved: the visible evidence cannot establish which grading criterion holds. Uncertainty itself is not proof of failure.
